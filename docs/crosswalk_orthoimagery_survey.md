# Public high-resolution orthoimagery for 17 Project Sidewalk cities

Desk survey, web only, 2026-11-01. Every ArcGIS endpoint named below was read via `?f=pjson` (or a catalog `query`) that day; no imagery was fetched. "Export cap" is the service's `maxImageWidth x maxImageHeight`; "no key" means the service answered anonymously. Anything not read on a primary page is marked **UNVERIFIED**. GSD conversions: 2 in = 5.08 cm, 3 in = 7.62 cm, 6 in = 15.24 cm.

Reference: Seattle's King County 3-inch (7.5 cm) tile cache is the model that worked. Ten of the other sixteen cities have a verified public source at <= 10 cm, five top out at 15 cm (county/state 6-inch programmes), and Santiago has nothing better than a 2012 16 cm mosaic.

### Seattle, WA (seattle-wa)
- Services: https://gismaps.kingcounty.gov/arcgis/rest/services/BaseMaps/KingCo_Aerial_2023/MapServer and https://gismaps.kingcounty.gov/arcgis/rest/services/BaseMaps/KingCo_Aerial_2025/MapServer .
