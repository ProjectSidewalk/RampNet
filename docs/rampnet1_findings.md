# RampNet 1.0: the findings, one line each

**Status: written 2026-09-21 against `main` at `081bde0`, after the mechanical closeout in
[#162](https://github.com/ProjectSidewalk/RampNet/issues/162) §A–E.** This page is the index
of every headline result RampNet 1.0 produced after the ICCV'25 workshop paper. Each row names
the number, the committed document that holds it, and the caveat that has to travel with it.
Every number below was re-read from the named document on the date above; this page is a map,
not a source. Where a number has been superseded, the current one is here and the old one is in
§3.

The long-form version, with motivation, method and the RampNet 2.0 case, is
[`rampnet1_report.md`](rampnet1_report.md).

| **Flip-TTA is not worth 2× GPU.** After the threshold drop, TTA's marginal effect over five US splits is +0.009 recall, −0.024 precision, −0.007 F1. | `operating_point.md` §TTA ([#78](https://github.com/ProjectSidewalk/RampNet/issues/78)); `figures/tta_operating_point.png` | In-domain is the exception: on manual_gold TTA keeps +0.019 recall at −0.015 precision and AP 0.904 → 0.917. | **Recall is distance-limited; precision is not.** Recall at 0.55 on richmond + bend (637 ramps): 0–8 m 0.842, 8–12 m 0.879, 12–18 m 0.812, **18–25 m 0.564, 25–40 m 0.182**. Precision is flat with distance (1.000 beyond 25 m); culling detections beyond 18 m would lose 132 true ramps to remove 4 false ones. | `detection_recall_analysis.md`; `figures/recall_hard_misses_richmond.png` | Two cities. Distance is flat-ground geometry at an assumed 2.5 m camera height, cross-checked against metric depth to 6.5–8.5%. GSV's own depth says that axis is stretched about 1.29–1.35× on paterson and gainesville because real camera heights are lower ([#112](https://github.com/ProjectSidewalk/RampNet/issues/112), RampNet 2.0); the ordering of the bands is unaffected, the metre labels are. |
| **Miss taxonomy: the levers are σ and calibration, not vocabulary.** Of 427 misses at 0.30 over the seven US splits: merged with a neighbour's peak 29.0%, sub-threshold 38.9%, mislocalized 2.1%, silent 30.0%. Of the 128 silent misses, only 8% have a flat heatmap; 62% are the tail of an adjacent mode and 30% a faint response on site. The population a broader training corpus could reach is **~0.013 recall points** (bracket 0.009–0.022), not the 0.087 first estimated. | `curb_ramp_data_sourcing.md` §0a–§0c ([#46](https://github.com/ProjectSidewalk/RampNet/issues/46)); `model_comparison.md` FP taxonomy | One rater, no second pass. `analysis_out/silent_activation.json` was produced once, on one local GPU ([#131](https://github.com/ProjectSidewalk/RampNet/issues/131)). The witness pool is frozen at the 2026-07-31 roster. 57.8% of misses are beyond 18 m; the far field is harder, not unreachable. |
| **Stage 2 training recipe.** The released checkpoint is the paper run's epoch 1, byte-identical; the paper run continued to 11.99 epochs and the rest was discarded. Re-run (Run A, 8 epochs, seed 42): epoch 1 is the only checkpoint clearly below the plateau (+0.007 to +0.011 F1 to reach it), **epochs 2–6 are indistinguishable, epochs 7–8 measurably decline** (3 vs 7 at z = 4.0). There is no resolvable human-labelled peak. | `stage2_epoch_curve_84.md` ([#84](https://github.com/ProjectSidewalk/RampNet/issues/84)); `stage2_run_b_power_135.md`; `stage2_training_cost.md`; `figures/stage2_epoch_curve_84.png`, `figures/stage2_manual_gold_curve_84.png` | Auto-label validation loss bottoms at epoch 5 in both runs and rises 4.6% by epoch 8 while human F1 moves 0.0055; the selection signal reports a decline human labels cannot see. Paired MDE on manual_gold is 0.0063. The code that produced the 2025 run is not in git; its per-epoch weights are gone; Run A's checkpoints live on klone, not on the Hub. |
| **Annealing does not reach human F1; Run B is not being run.** An 8-epoch cosine rung improves auto-label validation loss by up to 3.98% and moves manual_gold max-F1 by +0.0030 at epoch 8 (|z| 1.02–1.86, a tie). Decided 2026-09-03: a single 30-epoch run is n=1 and cannot be told from a seed draw; a readable Run B is three seeds, **5,025–10,050 GPU-hours, roughly $4,500–9,000**. | `stage2_cosine_rung_135.md` ([#135](https://github.com/ProjectSidewalk/RampNet/issues/135)) | The reopen condition (seed SD ≤ ~0.002 max-F1) was measured at 0.0030 (n=9) and is not met. | **Supervised transfer (Mapillary Vistas `Curb Cut`) transfers but does not compete.** On richmond at input parity (1024×1024): **F1 0.534** (P 0.383 / R 0.884 / AP 0.649) against RampNet 0.855; at the published 384×384 it was 0.517. It out-recalls RampNet (0.884 vs 0.768 at 0.55) and finds ~30 of RampNet's 53 misses after re-basing RampNet at 0.30 and discounting chance. A naive union loses (F1 0.555, ~8.2 FPs per recovered ramp). A confidence-gated cascade is live with a ceiling of **~19 ramps, +6.1 recall points**, at an FP cost that is unmeasured. | `model_comparison.md` §"Supervised transfer: Mapillary Vistas" ([#126](https://github.com/ProjectSidewalk/RampNet/issues/126), [#163](https://github.com/ProjectSidewalk/RampNet/issues/163)) | One split, one imagery tier. Vistas is a baseline, not a supervision source (the paper rejected its labels as too broad). Any complementarity read must re-base RampNet at 0.30, otherwise a third of the "recovered" ramps are ones RampNet already had. | **The 360° seam.** Three real defects: the scorer measured horizontal distance without wrapping (fixed, `eccadda`/`f4c71c8`); the gold set double-marks 10 seam ramps (adjudicated, not merged); Stage 1 double-labels **8,361 label pairs (0.98% of labels, 3.7% of panoramas)**, of which roughly one in seven is a genuine adjacent pair. The model's raw response is seam-sensitive: rolling the seam away moves 12 of 25 seam-band ramps by more than 0.05 (9 up, 3 down) against 0 of 77 controls. | `seam.md` ([#132](https://github.com/ProjectSidewalk/RampNet/issues/132), [#130](https://github.com/ProjectSidewalk/RampNet/issues/130)) | Two claims were retracted: "RampNet finds under half the ramps on the seam" was our own `exclude_border` bug (production recall is 0.96, 24 of 25), and "Stage 1 drops ~72% of labels near the seam" is the along-street density profile, identical at the anti-seam. Production and the paper's numbers were never affected. The 1.0 dataset is deliberately not being changed; the generator fix belongs in 2.0. The seam-response sample is n=25 and unstratified. Committed `op_cache` files predate the extractor fix and slightly understate recall. |
| **Laurens, IA: the deficit is the rig, not the town.** Same 1.91 km² footprint, two rigs: RampNet F1 **0.543** (R 0.390) on GoPro Max Mapillary, **0.659** (R 0.509) on GSV, +0.115. RampNet is 3–5× more rig-sensitive than a YOLO trained on its own data. | `model_comparison.md` §"The rig, not the town" ([#151](https://github.com/ProjectSidewalk/RampNet/issues/151)); `benchmark/README.md` Laurens section | Unpaired: the arms are different panorama sets (249 vs 220 GT ramps). | **Data scaling: 500k is not reachable on assessed data, and supply was never the constraint.** NYC is 78.2% of training records. "Composition beats volume" rests on the Paterson/Gainesville failure vocabularies not being NYC's. |

Qwen3-VL-32B falls below 8B (0.079 vs 0.169) by going nearly silent (0.24 boxes/pano). | `model_comparison.md` budapest section; `benchmark/README.md` | The reviewer rated their own pass low confidence, and imagery vs rubric cannot be separated on this split. ## 5. How to check this page

Every row's number comes from a committed artifact plus a committed script. The drift guards are
`python scripts/analysis/scoreboard.py --check`, `python scripts/analysis/operating_point_parity_51.py --check`,
`python scripts/analysis/seed_variance_read_51_135.py --check`, `python scripts/analysis/yolo_geometry_51.py --check`,
and `pytest -q` (which pins the scoreboard against `model_comparison.md`, the seed-variance read, the
power analysis and the seam fixes). Inputs a clean clone cannot obtain are listed per row in
[`replication.md`](replication.md).
