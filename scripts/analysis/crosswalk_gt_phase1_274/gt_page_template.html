Rubric (version __RUBRIC_VERSION__): the Phase 0 conventions, written down
  • Q1, painted crossing at the blue node. Y = a painted pedestrian crossing (zebra, ladder, two transverse lines, a coloured surface) passes through the node or within about 2 m of it (the inner ring), in either ortho year or the crowd's street-level crop. Faded but still visible paint is yes. R = painted crossing within the 5 m ring but clearly not at the node (the node sits beside it, or at the junction centre). N = no paint within 5 m in any image. U = the images cannot tell (trees, shadow, a vehicle or an imagery seam over the spot). An unmarked crossing (kerb cuts, no paint) is N, not U. T = a raised or textured crossing (a brick or stone table, a contrasting paver band) at the node with no paint; if it is also painted, answer Y or R.
  • Q1 toggles (record what you see; the scoring decides what counts). C: the node sits on bike-crossing paint (red Dutch surface, block-and-dash cycle markings, shark teeth). Answer Y/R/N for pedestrian paint only, then turn C on. V: the pedestrian paint is visible only in the crowd's street-level crop, in neither ortho year (removed since, or hidden). Q1 is still Y or R by the either-image rule above.
  • Q2, where the red crowd label sits (rows with a crowd label only). Judge the position ALONG the crossing; offset up or down the street is placement error. 1 on the paint at the centre. 2 near the centre: on or just off the paint, in line with the middle, not toward an end (in Seattle labels 3–5 m off were judged near; about 6 m off was off). 3 at a curb end of this crossing. 4 on a different crossing (another crossing or leg nearby, not the one at the node). 5 not on any crossing (mid-block, on a sidewalk or corner, far from any). 0 no paint to judge against (the node's crossing is unmarked, and the label is on it). For a T crossing, judge 1–3 against the raised or textured surface.
  • B: the crowd label sits on a bike crossing, not a pedestrian one (keep the position code against that bike crossing; the counts set these rows aside).
