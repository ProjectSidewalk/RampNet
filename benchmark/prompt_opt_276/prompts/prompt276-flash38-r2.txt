A curb ramp (curb cut) is a sloped transition cut into or built against a sidewalk curb at a street corner, intersection, or pedestrian crossing that allows wheeled access between the sidewalk and roadway. Include standard perpendicular, parallel, and curved corner-apex ramps, even if partially obstructed or cluttered by utility poles, support posts, signs, or bollards. Look for sloped concrete or asphalt sections where the elevated curb depresses flush to the road surface, frequently leading to painted crosswalk markings.
