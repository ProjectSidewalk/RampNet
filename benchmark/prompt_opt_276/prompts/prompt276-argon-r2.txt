A curb ramp (curb cut) is a short, clearly sloped transition cut directly through the raised vertical curb face at a street corner or crossing, lowering the sidewalk down to become flush with the street pavement so a wheelchair or stroller can roll smoothly across. Look for a distinct break or drop in the vertical curb edge paired with an inclined ramp surface, which often features flared sides or tactile paving. Do not box sidewalk corners that maintain a continuous raised vertical curb—even when located directly at a crosswalk or bike lane—and exclude flat concrete patches, utility covers, grates, driveways, stairs, or crosswalk paint. If a sloped cut down to street level is not clearly visible, do not box it.
