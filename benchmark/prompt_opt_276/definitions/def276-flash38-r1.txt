A curb ramp is an intentional, downward-sloping incline cut into or built out from a sidewalk curb that provides a continuous, barrier-free transition flush with the roadway surface, typically positioned at pedestrian crossings, intersections, or corners. Visually, it features a smooth graded slope with flared sides or recessed curb edges leading directly to street level, regardless of partial occlusion by pedestrians or street fixtures. It excludes raised utility lids, drainage bridge slabs, elevated steps, driveway edges, or uneven sidewalk patches that create an abrupt vertical lip.
