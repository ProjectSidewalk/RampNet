A curb ramp is a sloped transition or lowered section of sidewalk curb at a corner, intersection, or crossing that enables smooth passage between sidewalk and street level. It may be straight or wrap along a curved corner radius, and its surface or edges may be partially occupied by or adjacent to utility poles, posts, or infrastructure. Detectable features include a distinct downward incline toward the roadway, a flush or depressed curb lip, and frequent alignment with crosswalk markings or pedestrian paths.
