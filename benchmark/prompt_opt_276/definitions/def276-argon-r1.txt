A curb ramp (curb cut) is a sloped transition cut into or formed along a sidewalk curb at a street corner or crosswalk that lets wheelchairs or strollers roll smoothly between the sidewalk and street. It includes both distinct cut-out ramps with flared sides and subtle, gradual sections where the curb edge tapers down flush with the road surface, even without tactile paving. Do not confuse it with rectangular concrete storm-drain covers or catch basins built into the curb edge, which stay level with the sidewalk and have a drainage opening underneath.
