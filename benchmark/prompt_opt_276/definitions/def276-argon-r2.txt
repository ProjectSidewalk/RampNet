A curb ramp (curb cut) is a short sloped ramp cut into a sidewalk curb at a street corner or crossing that lets a wheelchair or stroller roll smoothly between the sidewalk and street. Visually, it features an inclined plane flanked by flared sides or drop curbs that interrupts the vertical curb face to meet the roadway flush at street level. Do not confuse it with an intact, un-lowered sidewalk corner, utility covers, or tactile paving where a continuous vertical step-down curb edge remains unbroken along the perimeter.
