Richmond flat photos (#218): detections

Each card is a crop of one Richmond flat (non-360) Mapillary photo, with a ring on a RampNet detection (canvas-embed arm, score at least 0.30). Answer one question: Is there a curb ramp at the ring?

Optionally, if you can see the curb ramp the ring seems to be aiming at, click it on the image, whatever your answer (a magenta mark appears; click again to move it). Leave it unmarked if there is none.

Yes
A curb ramp is at the ring or touching it: a sloped section that takes the sidewalk down through the curb to street level (Project Sidewalk: 'a curb ramp connecting sidewalk to street'). It may be partly hidden or far away, as long as you can see that it is a ramp.
No
No curb ramp at the ring: plain curb, sidewalk, street, a driveway (Project Sidewalk puts no Curb Ramp label on driveways), a level crossing with no curb, or something else; and the nearest ramp, if any, is more than roughly one ramp width away.
Can't tell
The crop does not let you decide (too dark, blocked, too blurry, too far). Excluded from every rate.
