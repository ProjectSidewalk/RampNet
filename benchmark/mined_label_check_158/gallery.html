Yes
A curb ramp is at the ring or touching it. It may be partly hidden or faint, as long as you can see it is a ramp.
No
There is no curb ramp at that spot: the ring is on plain curb, a driveway, sidewalk, street or something else, and the nearest ramp (if any) is more than roughly one ramp width away.
Can't tell
The view does not let you decide (too dark, blocked, too far, too blurry). Excluded from every rate.
