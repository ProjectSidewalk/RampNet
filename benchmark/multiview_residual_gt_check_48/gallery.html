The smaller views on the right are other captures of the same corner, shown only as context, without a ring (a projected ring would often miss by metres), and nothing is rated from them.

Yes
A curb ramp is at the ring or touching it. The ramp may be partly hidden or faint, as long as you can see it is a ramp.
No
There is no curb ramp at that spot: the ring is on plain curb, sidewalk, street or something else, and the nearest ramp (if any) is more than roughly one ramp width away. This means the GT point is wrong or misplaced.
Can't tell
The source view does not let you decide (too dark, blocked, too far, too blurry). Excluded from every rate.
